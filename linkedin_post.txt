🚀 Excited to share my latest Data Science & Business Intelligence project with Future Interns! 📊

As part of my Data Science Internship (Task 1), I analyzed 540,000+ retail sales transactions totaling $10.67M in revenue to build an end-to-end Executive Sales Intelligence Dashboard & Strategic Advisory Report.

🔍 Key Questions Solved:
1️⃣ How does revenue evolve over time? → Uncovered massive Q4 holiday seasonality peaking at $1.51M in Nov (+98.2% MoM).
2️⃣ Which products drive the business? → Validated the Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule): Top 19.8% of SKUs generate 80% of total revenue!
3️⃣ Where are international growth opportunities? → International markets (Netherlands, Germany, France) boast an Average Order Value 3.2x higher than domestic UK orders ($3,000+ vs $500).
4️⃣ How to optimize customer retention? → Segmented 4,338 customers using RFM analysis, identifying $1.45M in at-risk customer revenue for automated win-back workflows.

🛠️ Tools & Stack Used:
• Python (Pandas, NumPy) for data cleaning and pipeline engineering
• Plotly, Seaborn & Matplotlib for publication-grade 300 DPI visualizations
• Streamlit & Tailwind CSS / HTML5 for standalone interactive dashboards
• RFM Customer Segmentation & Pareto Analysis
