Advisory Blueprint

Actionable Executive Recommendations for Business Growth

Data-driven strategic growth roadmap synthesized from transaction patterns, customer RFM scoring, and international demand.

1

Capitalize on Q4 Holiday Surge with Early Supply Chain Locking

Observation: Sales spike exponentially starting September and peak in November ($1.51M, +98% MoM), driven by holiday gift items, party supplies, and decorations.
Recommendation: Finalize supplier contracts and inventory buffers by late August to avoid stockouts on top-selling decor items like Regency Cakestand and White Hanging Heart Holder.

2

Implement Pareto-Driven Inventory Optimization

Observation: The top 19.8% of product SKUs generate 80.0% of total revenue. A massive long tail of slow-moving items ties up working capital.
Recommendation: Adopt ABC inventory categorization. Prioritize automated re-order triggers and prime warehouse placement for Class-A items, while running clearance flash sales on zero-velocity SKUs.

3

Expand European B2B Wholesale Hubs

Observation: International buyers in the Netherlands ($285k), EIRE ($283k), Germany ($228k), and France ($209k) exhibit order values up to $2,900+ (5.4x UK average), representing lucrative wholesale accounts.
Recommendation: Build dedicated localized wholesale portals, offer tiered bulk pricing (e.g., discounts on >50 units), and partner with regional EU 3PL fulfillment centers to reduce transit times and shipping costs.

4

Automate RFM Customer Retention & Win-Back Drips

Observation: 628 high-value customers (14.5%) are in the 'At Risk' segment, having not ordered in over 3 months despite past high spending.
Recommendation: Implement automated marketing triggers: at 60 days inactivity, send a personalized product recommendation email; at 90 days, send an exclusive VIP 15% incentive code.
